About Us

Organizing San Diego

The Working Families Party fights for economic, racial, and climate justice: workers over bosses, people over the powerful. We organize on top of and around the two-party system to elect champions for working families and win bold policies that put communities first.

WFP San Diego is the regional chapter of the California Working Families Party. We are part of a statewide multiracial coalition of grassroots organizations and labor unions, building real governing power from school boards and water districts to Congress.

From rising rents to corporate price gouging, families across our region deserve leaders with the backbone to fight for health care and housing for all, fully funded schools, and a California that works for everyone, in the coastal cities, East County, and the South Bay alike.
